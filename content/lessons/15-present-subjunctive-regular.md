# Present subjunctive regular

The subjunctive follows expressions of wish, emotion, doubt and purpose, usually after *que*. *Quiero que vengas* means "I want you to come". It also follows *cuando* when it refers to the future.

## Forms

Start from the yo form of the present and take off the -o. -ar verbs then take -e endings, and -er and -ir verbs take -a endings.

## Examples

- *Quiero que hables con él.* I want you to talk to him.
- *Es normal que aprendas despacio.* It's normal that you learn slowly.
- *Ojalá vivan cerca.* I hope they live nearby.
- *Espero que me llames.* I hope you call me.
