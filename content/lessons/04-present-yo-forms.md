# Present yo forms

Some verbs are irregular in the yo form of the present. The other persons follow the regular pattern.

## Forms

- **-go**: hacer (hago), poner (pongo), salir (salgo), traer (traigo)
- **-zco**: conocer (conozco). Most verbs that end in a vowel + -cer or -cir work the same way, such as parecer (parezco).
- **Saber** has *sé*.

## Examples

- *Hago la compra los lunes.* I do the shopping on Mondays.
- *Salgo de casa a las nueve.* I leave home at nine.
- *No conozco a tu hermana.* I don't know your sister.
- *No sé la respuesta.* I don't know the answer.
- *Vengo mañana.* I'm coming tomorrow.
- *¿Qué dices?* What are you saying?
