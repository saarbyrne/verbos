# Negative imperative

Negative commands use **no** + the present subjunctive in every person.

## Forms

{{table hablar comer ir "Presente Imperativo Negativo"}}

- Irregular forms come from the subjunctive: *no tengas*, *no hagas*, *no vayas*, *no seas*, *no digas*.
- Pronouns go before the verb: *no te preocupes*, *no me lo digas*.
- The quiz accepts the answer with or without *no*.

## Examples

- *No hables tan alto.* Don't talk so loud.
- *No tengas miedo.* Don't be afraid.
- *No vayáis solos.* Don't go on your own (to a group).
- *No me digas.* You don't say.
