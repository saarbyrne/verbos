# Future

The future is for plans, predictions and guesses about the present: *estará en casa* means "he's probably at home". In speech, **ir a** + infinitive is more common for plans.

## Forms

Add the endings to the whole infinitive. Every verb uses the same endings: -é, -ás, -á, -emos, -éis, -án.

{{table hablar comer vivir "Futuro Indicativo"}}

Some verbs have an irregular stem, but the endings stay the same.

- **The vowel drops**: poder (podr-), saber (sabr-), querer (querr-), haber (habr-)
- **A d replaces the vowel**: tener (tendr-), poner (pondr-), salir (saldr-), venir (vendr-)
- **A short stem**: hacer (har-), decir (dir-)

## Examples

- *Mañana lloverá.* It will rain tomorrow.
- *Te llamaré luego.* I'll call you later.
- *¿Podrás venir?* Will you be able to come?
- *Lo haremos el lunes.* We'll do it on Monday.
