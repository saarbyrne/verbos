# Present core irregulars

These are the most common verbs in Spanish, and they do not follow the regular pattern. Learn them as whole tables.

- **Ser** is for identity, origin, jobs and time. **Estar** is for location and temporary states.
- **Ir a** + infinitive talks about the near future. *Voy a comer* means "I'm going to eat".
- **Haber** builds the perfect tenses (*he comido*). Its special form *hay* means "there is" or "there are". The quiz accepts *hay* for the third person singular.

## Examples

- *Soy de Irlanda.* I'm from Ireland.
- *Estamos cansados.* We are tired.
- *Vamos al mercado.* We're going to the market.
- *Tengo dos hermanos.* I have two brothers.
- *Hay un problema.* There is a problem.
- *No veo nada.* I can't see anything.
