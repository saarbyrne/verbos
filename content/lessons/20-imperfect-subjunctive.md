# Imperfect subjunctive

The imperfect subjunctive is the subjunctive in past contexts and in "if" sentences: *quería que vinieras* means "I wanted you to come", and *si tuviera dinero, viajaría* means "if I had money, I would travel".

## Forms

Take the ellos form of the preterite, remove -ron, and add -ra, -ras, -ra, -ramos, -rais, -ran. The nosotros form has an accent: *habláramos*.

Every preterite irregularity carries over: tuvieron → *tuviera*, hicieron → *hiciera*, fueron → *fuera* (for ser and ir).

The -se forms (*hablase*, *tuviese*) mean the same thing. The quiz accepts both.

## Examples

- *Si tuviera tiempo, iría.* If I had time, I would go.
- *Me pidió que le ayudara.* He asked me to help him.
- *Ojalá estuvieras aquí.* I wish you were here.
- *Quería que dijeras la verdad.* I wanted you to tell the truth.
