# Present regular

The present is for things happening now, habits and facts.

## Forms

Take off -ar, -er or -ir to get the stem. Then add the ending for the person.

{{table hablar comer vivir "Presente Indicativo"}}

- -er and -ir verbs share every ending except nosotros and vosotros.
- Spanish usually drops the subject pronoun, because the ending already shows the person. *Hablo* means "I speak".

## Examples

- *Trabajo en casa.* I work at home.
- *¿Comes carne?* Do you eat meat?
- *Vivimos en España.* We live in Spain.
- *Mis padres escriben correos largos.* My parents write long emails.
