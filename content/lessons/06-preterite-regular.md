# Preterite regular

The preterite (indefinido) is for finished actions at a specific time in the past, often with words like *ayer*, *la semana pasada* or *en 2020*.

## Forms

{{table hablar comer vivir "Pretérito Indicativo"}}

- -er and -ir verbs share all their endings.
- The yo and él forms have an accent: *hablé*, *habló*. Without the accent, *hablo* is the present.
- The nosotros form of -ar and -ir verbs is the same as in the present (*hablamos*, *vivimos*). The context shows which one it is.

## Examples

- *Ayer trabajé diez horas.* Yesterday I worked ten hours.
- *¿Comiste bien?* Did you eat well?
- *Vivimos en Madrid dos años.* We lived in Madrid for two years.
- *Me llamaron por la mañana.* They called me in the morning.
