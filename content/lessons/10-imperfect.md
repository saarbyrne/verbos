# Imperfect

The imperfect is for the background of a story, habits in the past and descriptions. It covers what was going on or what used to happen. The preterite covers events that finished.

## Forms

{{table hablar comer vivir "Imperfecto Indicativo"}}

- The yo and él forms are the same: *hablaba*.
- The nosotros form of -ar verbs has an accent: *hablábamos*.
- -er and -ir verbs have í in every form: *comía*.

Only three verbs are irregular.

## Examples

- *Vivíamos en Dublín.* We used to live in Dublin.
- *Cuando era pequeño, iba al colegio en bici.* When I was little, I went to school by bike.
- *Estaba cansado.* I was tired.
- *Veíamos la tele cada noche.* We watched TV every night.
