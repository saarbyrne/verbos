# Present perfect

The present perfect is haber in the present plus the participle. In Spain it is used for past actions in a time period that includes now, such as *hoy*, *esta semana*, *este año* or *alguna vez*.

## Participle

-ar verbs take -ado. -er and -ir verbs take -ido.

These participles are irregular: hacer (hecho), decir (dicho), ver (visto), poner (puesto), escribir (escrito), volver (vuelto), abrir (abierto), romper (roto), morir (muerto).

- The participle does not change with the person.
- Reflexive and object pronouns go before haber: *me he levantado*.

## Examples

- *Hoy he terminado pronto.* Today I finished early.
- *¿Has visto mis gafas?* Have you seen my glasses?
- *Hemos vuelto esta mañana.* We came back this morning.
- *No me han dicho nada.* They haven't told me anything.
