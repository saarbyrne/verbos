# Present subjunctive irregular

Most irregular subjunctives come from the yo form of the present. If the yo form is irregular, the whole subjunctive uses that stem: tengo → *tenga*, hago → *haga*, digo → *diga*, conozco → *conozca*.

## Forms

- Stem-changing verbs change in the same persons as in the present: *piense*, but *pensemos*.
- -ir stem-changing verbs also change in nosotros and vosotros: *pidamos*, *durmamos*.
- Spelling changes keep the sound: buscar → *busque*, llegar → *llegue*, empezar → *empiece*.

## Examples

- *Espero que tengas suerte.* I hope you're lucky.
- *No creo que sea verdad.* I don't think it's true.
- *Quiero que vayas tú.* I want you to go.
- *Ojalá haya sitio.* I hope there's room.
