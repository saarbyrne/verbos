# Compound subjunctive

The perfect subjunctive is haber in the present subjunctive plus the participle: *espero que hayas dormido bien*, "I hope you slept well".

{{table hablar hacer "Presente perfecto Subjuntivo"}}

The pluperfect subjunctive is haber in the imperfect subjunctive plus the participle. It is common in "if" sentences about the past: *si hubiera sabido, habría venido*, "if I had known, I would have come".

{{table hablar hacer "Pluscuamperfecto Subjuntivo"}}

The quiz also accepts *hubiese* for *hubiera*.

## Examples

- *Me alegro de que hayas venido.* I'm glad you've come.
- *Cuando hayas terminado, llámame.* When you've finished, call me.
- *Si hubieras llegado antes, lo habrías visto.* If you had arrived earlier, you would have seen it.
- *Ojalá hubiéramos ido.* I wish we had gone.
