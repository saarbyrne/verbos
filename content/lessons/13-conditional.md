# Conditional

The conditional means "would". It is also used for polite requests and for guesses about the past.

## Forms

Add -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían to the whole infinitive.

## Examples

- *Me gustaría un café.* I would like a coffee.
- *¿Podrías ayudarme?* Could you help me?
- *Yo en tu lugar no diría nada.* In your place I wouldn't say anything.
- *Sería mejor salir pronto.* It would be better to leave early.
