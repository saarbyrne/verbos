# Preterite irregular

Many common verbs have an irregular stem in the preterite. They use one special set of endings with no accents: -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron.

## Forms

- tener → **tuv-**, estar → **estuv-**, poder → **pud-**, poner → **pus-**
- hacer → **hic-** (the él form is *hizo*), querer → **quis-**, venir → **vin-**
- decir → **dij-** and traer → **traj-**. Stems that end in j take -eron in the ellos form: *dijeron*, *trajeron*.

Ser and ir share the same forms, and the context shows which verb it is. Dar takes the -er and -ir endings, without accents.

## Examples

- *Tuve un problema con el coche.* I had a problem with the car.
- *¿Qué hiciste el fin de semana?* What did you do at the weekend?
- *Fuimos a la playa.* We went to the beach.
- *Me dijeron que no.* They told me no.
- *No pude venir.* I couldn't come.
