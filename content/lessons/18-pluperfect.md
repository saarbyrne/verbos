# Pluperfect

The pluperfect means "had done". It is for an action that happened before another past action: *cuando llegué, ya se había ido* means "when I arrived, he had already left".

## Forms

Haber in the imperfect (había, habías, había, habíamos, habíais, habían) plus the participle.

It uses the same irregular participles as the present perfect.

## Examples

- *Nunca había visto el mar.* I had never seen the sea.
- *Ya habíamos comido.* We had already eaten.
- *Me dijo que había escrito la carta.* She told me she had written the letter.
